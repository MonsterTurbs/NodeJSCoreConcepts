🛠️ Don’t Block the Worker Pool

❓ What Is “Blocking”?

  • 🛑Blocking means your code keeps scheduling tasks so fast that the worker pool’s queue grows huge.
  • ⏳Long‐running tasks fill every thread; then tiny tasks must wait for all big ones to finish before running.

⚠️ Why Blocking Is Bad

  • 🐢Users with quick requests (milliseconds) get stuck waiting minutes or hours until heavy tasks finish.
  • 💥Server performance degrades; memory may spike and crash the app.
  • 🚫Denial-of-Service risk: attackers submit many heavy tasks to starve out normal requests.

🔍 Common Blocking Scenarios

  • 🔒Scheduling millions of CPU‐heavy jobs (e.g., large file processing, big computations) without batching.
  • 🧩Using slow NPM modules or regex patterns that block threads unpredictably.
  • 📦JSON.parse/stringify on enormous payloads blocks both event loop and worker pool.

🚀 How to Avoid Blocking

  • 📦Batching: split huge jobs into smaller batches so workers finish in bursts and free up threads.
  • 🗄️Caching: reuse previous results rather than recomputing every time.
  • 📡Offload: send very long tasks (video/audio processing) to dedicated services or servers.
  • ⛔️Rate-limit: throttle per-user or per-IP to prevent flood of heavy tasks.
