🏁 Understanding Race Conditions

⚡ What Is a Race Condition?

  • 🏃‍♂️Occurs when multiple threads/processes read & write the same data at once.
  • ❓Final result depends on who writes last—“the winner” of the race.

🔍 Example Scenario

  • 🔢Start with a shared number = 0.
  • ➕Two threads each do: read, add 1, write back, 500 times.
  • ⚠️Instead of ending at 1000, you get less because reads overlap.

🎯 Critical Section

  • 🔐The code block that reads then writes shared data is called the critical section.
  • 🚧Must prevent two threads entering it simultaneously.

🐢 Why It Happens

  • ⏸️Thread A reads value, gets paused before writing.
  • 🏃Thread B reads the same old value, writes its update.
  • 🔄Thread A resumes and overwrites B’s update—lost increment.

🛠️ Preventing Race Conditions

  • 🔒Locks / Mutexes: Only one thread may enter critical section at a time.
  • ⚙️Atomic Operations: Read‐modify‐write done as one uninterruptible step.
