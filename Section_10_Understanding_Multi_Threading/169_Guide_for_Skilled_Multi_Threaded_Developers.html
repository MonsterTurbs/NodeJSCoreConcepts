🚀 Spawning a Worker

  • Import: const { Worker } = require('worker_threads')
  • Start: new Worker('path/to/file.js')
  • Send data:
    worker.postMessage(data)
  • Receive data:
    worker.on('message', msg => {…})

🔗 MessageChannel & SharedArrayBuffer

  • MessageChannel & MessagePort for multi-port comms.
  • SharedArrayBuffer + Atomics for shared memory.
  • Manual layout: break objects into TypedArrays or Buffers.

⚠️ Sync Issues: Atomics & Locks

  • Use Atomics.wait & Atomics.notify to block/resume threads.
  • Build mutexes/semaphores with Atomics.compareExchange, Atomics.add.
  • Avoid race conditions & deadlocks—test heavily!
