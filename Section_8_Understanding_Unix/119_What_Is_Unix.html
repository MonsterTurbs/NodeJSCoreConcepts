🐧 What Is Unix?

❓Definition

  • Unix is an operating system first made in the late 1960s/early 1970s.
  • Created by Ken Thompson & Dennis Ritchie at Bell Labs (AT&T).
  • Powerful, portable, and inspired many modern OSes.

🏛️Bell Labs & Inventors

  • Bell Labs: Research center behind many tech advances.
  • Dennis Ritchie: Invented C language; rewrote Unix in C for portability.
  • Ken Thompson: Co-creator of original Unix in assembly language.

🎯Unix Philosophy

  • 🛠️Modular design: small programs that each do one job well.
  • 🔗Pipes & IPC: programs communicate by streaming data.
  • 📜Scripting: combine tools in shell scripts for powerful workflows.

🌐Unix “Descendants”

  • Linux (by Linus Torvalds): Rewritten from scratch in C, open source.
  • BSD (FreeBSD, etc.): Direct branch of original Unix code.
  • macOS (Darwin): Apple’s Unix-based system from BSD.
  • Android: Linux-based for mobile devices.
  • iOS: Apple’s Unix-like kernel for iPhones/iPads.
