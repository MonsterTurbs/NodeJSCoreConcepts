🔒Understanding TLS

🛡️What it is: TLS (Transport Layer Security) is like a secret tunnel for data on the internet—keeping messages private and safe from tampering.

🚀Why use it: Ensures that when you visit websites or send info online, nobody else can read or change your data.

🔑Key Steps in a TLS Handshake:

  • 👋Client Hello: Your browser says “Hello” and shares what it supports.
  • 🤝Server Hello: Server replies, picks settings, and sends its digital certificate.
  • 🔍Certificate Check: Browser checks the certificate is valid and trusted.
  • 🔐Key Exchange: Both sides agree on secret keys using asymmetric math.
  • ✅Secure Connection: All further messages are encrypted with those agreed keys.
