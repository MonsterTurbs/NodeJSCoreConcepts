✍️Digital Signatures

🖊️What it is: A way to prove that a message really came from you and hasn't been changed.

🔒Why use it: Ensures authenticity, integrity, and that you can't deny you sent it.

🎓What you'll learn:

  • 📑How a private key creates a signature
  • 🔑How a public key checks it
  • 🤝Real-world uses: e-contracts, email, software updates
