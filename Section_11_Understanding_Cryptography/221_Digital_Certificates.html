🎟️Digital Certificates

📝What it is: A digital ID card that proves a website or person is who they say they are.

🔐Why use it: Ensures secure connections (HTTPS) and trust between browsers and servers.

🎯Key points:

  • 🏛️Issued by Certificate Authorities (CAs)
  • 🔎Contains public key, owner info, and expiration date
  • ✔️Browser checks CA signature before trusting
