🍪 HTTP Cookies – Beginner Notes

📬 What Are Cookies?

  • 📦Small pieces of data servers ask browsers to save.
  • 🔗Browsers then include them automatically in every request.
  • 🌐Used to track sessions, preferences, shopping carts…

✉️ Sending a Cookie

  • ➡️Server sets header in **response**: 
    Set-Cookie: token=ABC123;
  • ⚙️One cookie per header—send multiple headers for multiple cookies.

💾 Browser Saves It

  • 💿Browser stores cookies on disk by default.
  • 🗂️Available even after tab or computer restart.

📥 Returning Cookies

  • 🔁Browser sends back in **request** header: 
    Cookie: token=ABC123; cartId=XYZ789
  • ⚠️All cookies go in one header, separated by `; `.

⚙️ Cookie Attributes

  • ⏰Expires/Max-Age: when cookie dies.
  • 🔒HttpOnly: not accessible to JavaScript.
  • 🔐Secure: send only over HTTPS.
  • 🛣️Path: restrict which URLs include it.
  • 🌍Domain: restrict to specific domains/subdomains.

🚧 Performance & Security

  • 📈Cookies sent with every request—keep size small!
  • 🔍Don’t store sensitive data directly—store a token only.
  • 🔄On logout, send back Set-Cookie with past expiry to delete.

🔗 More Info

  • 📚Read full spec & attributes on MDN:
    • https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Cookies
  • 💡Alternatives: use localStorage or sessionStorage for JS-managed data.
