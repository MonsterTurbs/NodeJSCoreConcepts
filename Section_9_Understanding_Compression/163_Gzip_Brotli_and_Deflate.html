🛠️ Benchmarking gzip, Brotli & Deflate

🔗 zlib & Brotli Dependencies

  • Node’s zlib module wraps two C‐libs: zlib (gzip/deflate) and brotli 💪
  • Use zlib.createGzip(), zlib.createDeflate() and zlib.createBrotliCompress() streams

📑 Benchmark Script

  • Loop over a files/ folder, compress each with gzip, deflate or Brotli 🔄
  • Measure time (via performance.now()) and ratio (origSize ÷ newSize) 📊
  • Play with options:
    • level / quality (1–9 or 0–11) → speed ↔ ratio ⚖️
    • windowBits → memory/trade-off 🔍

⚖️ Results & Trade-Offs

  • Repetitive data (AAAA…) → huge ratios (×1000+) but simple custom method could beat all ❕
  • Unique data (ABCD…) → compressing can enlarge it → skip compression 🚫
  • JSON/text often compresses (×4–5) in ms → worthwhile 👍
  • Speed vs ratio: more CPU → better shrink, but costs time → pick balance for your app ⏱️

💡 Default Advice

  • For a simple web server: use gzip with default options (broad browser support) 🌐
  • For heavy traffic or large assets: benchmark your real data, tune level/quality and consider Brotli 💼
  • Always test on single-core basis to compare true CPU cost & latency ⚙️
