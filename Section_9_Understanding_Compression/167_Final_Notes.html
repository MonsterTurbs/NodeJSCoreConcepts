🔧 Final Points on Compression

🏁 1. Recap & Docs

  • Review Node’s zlib docs: gzip, deflate, brotli streams & buffer APIs 📚
  • Check constants and thread-pool notes (default 4 threads) 🧵
  • Explore new APIs: zlib.crc32() for checksums 🔢

⚠️ 2. Thread-Pool Caution

  • Compression offloads to libuv thread-pool (default 4 threads) ⚙️
  • Spawning thousands of compress calls can exhaust threads → crash 💥
  • Queue or rate-limit compress tasks on high-traffic servers ⏳

💾 3. Cache Compressed Results

  • Compression is CPU-heavy – avoid repeating work 🔄
  • Cache compressed payloads (in memory, Redis, disk) for unchanged content 🗄️
  • Serve cached version to thousands of clients without recompressing ✅
